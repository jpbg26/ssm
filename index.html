Artist catalog coverage

Expansion uses top-10 song placements from 1970 through available 2026 charts, including credited guests and groups. Album positions do not qualify. Earlier editorial references remain available.

Coverage is not exhaustive: the Hot 100 archive reaches October 17, 2026; country and adult-format archives reach June 2025; rock, alternative and R&B archives reach August 2024; Latin, dance and pop-airplay imports end in 2018. Christian and gospel additions include documented chart summaries. Some credited names need further identity review. These gaps prevent a complete all-genres-through-2026 claim.
